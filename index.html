Hi.

2,000+reported hi's and counting

In October 2026 a drawing went up on X with one question underneath it: ever dream this cat?

The replies did not say “cute”. They said “wait.” They said “I know him.” Not from a feed, not from a pet shop window, but from their sleep.

The details never match. A kitchen that wasn’t theirs. A bus that kept not stopping. An exam hall with no exam. But the cat is always there, a little too close, eyes far too big, and it always says the same single word: “Hi.”

It never says anything else. It does not answer questions. Nobody has ever seen it blink.

The only lead so far is an account on X whose name is Hi, whose bio is Hi, and whose profile picture is, unmistakably, the cat. We have written to it. It has not written back.

thishi.org exists to collect every reported hi, to put the people who have received one in touch with each other, and to work out, if it can be worked out, why a ginger cat is visiting strangers at night just to greet them.

If this cat has said hi to you, tell us about it.
