Nothing below has been tested. These are only possible explanations for why someone might dream of this cat, grouped the same way thisman.org groups its theories.

Archetype theory

A big-eyed, friendly animal is the kind of image the mind may reach for on its own. If so, the cat would not be a specific cat at all, just a familiar shape the brain uses for “someone is here”.

Religious theory

Some people read recurring dream figures as messengers. Under this view, a cat whose only word is “Hi” is delivering the message in full.

Dream surfer theory

The least likely option: a real cat that can move between dreams. There is no evidence for this, and the cat has not commented.

Dream imitation theory

Hearing about a dream makes people more likely to have it. If the poster spreads, reports may follow because people have seen the poster.

Daytime recognition theory

Faces in dreams are hard to remember. Someone who sees this picture may match it to a vague dream figure after the fact. Of the five, this is the easiest explanation to test, and it could account for any reports this site receives.
