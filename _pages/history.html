History

Nobody knows when the first person dreamed of the cat. Everybody remembers when they found out they weren’t the only one.

  1. Before 2026

    Scattered nights

    People wake up with the vague memory of a ginger cat, uncomfortably close, saying hi. Most of them assume it means nothing. A few write it down. None of them mention it to anyone, because what would you even say.

  2. 2026

    An account called Hi

    An X account appears under the handle @SillyPianist67. Display name: Hi. Bio: Hi. Profile picture: the cat. Dreamers who come across it describe a strong urge to close the app.

  3. October 2026

    The poster

    A sketch of the face goes up on X with the caption ever dream this cat? It spreads the way these things do: slowly, then all at once. The replies fill with people who had kept quiet about it for years.

  4. 18 October 2026

    thishi.org

    This site goes online to gather the reports in one place. Within its first day it logs more hi’s than its founders had expected in a year.

  5. Now

    Still saying hi

    The cat keeps turning up. It has not changed its line. Has it visited you?
