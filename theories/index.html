Theories

Why would thousands of strangers dream of the same cat, and why would it only ever say one word? These are the explanations people keep sending us. None of them has been ruled out. None of them has been ruled in either.

1. The archetype theory

Somewhere in the shared basement of the human mind sits a standard-issue Friendly Presence. For some people it is a grandparent; for others, a lighthouse. For an alarming number, it is a ginger cat with dinner-plate eyes. “Hi” is simply the shortest possible way of saying you are not alone in here.

2. The pianist theory

The only account known to be connected to the cat is called SillyPianist67. Supporters believe the cat is a musician, and that “Hi” is not a word but a note: the one it has been practising for years, played into the dreams of anyone who happens to be in tune.

3. The broadcast theory

There is one real cat, somewhere, that says hi so sincerely the greeting leaks out of the waking world and into the sleeping one. Under this theory, every dream is a rebroadcast. Nobody has managed to find the transmitter.

4. The dream-walking theory

The cat walks from dream to dream the way cats walk from room to room: without asking, and with complete confidence. It says hi because it is polite. It leaves before you can reply because it is a cat.

5. The timeline theory

The sceptic’s favourite: you saw the picture while scrolling half-asleep, forgot about it, and your brain served it back at 3 a.m. Neat. It does not explain the people who say they dreamed of the cat before the picture existed.

6. The it’s-just-a-cat theory

It is just a cat. It is saying hi. Perhaps the correct response is to say hi back.

Have a better theory? Send it in and we’ll consider it, carefully, while trying not to fall asleep.
